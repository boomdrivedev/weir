specs) and building on the tasks before each. Record 0010, core.54 to
core.57 and the amended core.4, core.6, core.13, core.14, core.16, core.20
and core.33 are on this branch. The statement is read at blueprints
`30dce35`: 117 to 129, C.1, C.2, C.5 and C.9 as written there. No source,
test or fixture names a tool of the family (core.2): the people and the
rules are C.9's example, kept once as `crates/weir-policy/tests/c9.toml`
and read by weir-capture's and weir-signals' tests through
`include_str!`. No test reaches a network. A test named for a scenario
holds that scenario and keeps its name, in snake case. Each check runs
one crate's tests, one of its test binaries or one test, through that
crate's moon task, moon passing what follows `--` to `cargo test`. The
task groups are headings for reading. The verify stage runs the
repository's whole checks once every task is done.
